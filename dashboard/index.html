Readings that are not physically possible. Technical Building & Tech Building Old on 29 Jun 2026 (-223.29 kWh, -120%), 10 Sep 2026 (252.83 kWh, 143%), 17 Sep 2026 (-144.81 kWh, -101%), 18 Sep 2026 (102.59 kWh, 105%). An array cannot generate negative energy, nor convert more sunlight than fell on it, so these are meter or data-entry faults rather than performance. A negative reading is subtracted from that day's plant total, so the complex's reported generation is understated on those days too. Daily figures are usually the difference between two cumulative meter readings, and that difference turns negative when the meter is reset, replaced, rolls over, or when two readings are recorded out of order — which also produces an impossibly high day on the other side of the swap. They are left in the dataset exactly as the sheet reported them; nothing here is silently corrected.
2 sites report the same figure every single day — Bank & Post Office at 41.67 kWh, Admin Cluster & Administrative Building at 9.00 kWh. Solar output follows the weather, so an identical reading on a clear day and on one with no recorded sunshine is a fixed value in the sheet, not a measurement. They are marked fixed value in the list above; plotting one gives a curve that traces the day's sunshine upside-down rather than anything the array did. Their generation is still counted in the plant total.
