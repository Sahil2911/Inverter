3 sites report the same figure every single day — Bank & Post Office at 41.67 kWh, Admin Cluster & Administrative Building at 9.00 kWh, Get Hostel 1&2 at 0.40 kWh. Solar output follows the weather, so an identical reading on a clear day and on one with no recorded sunshine is a fixed value in the sheet, not a measurement. They are marked fixed value in the list above; plotting one gives a curve that traces the day's sunshine upside-down rather than anything the array did. Their generation is still counted in the plant total.
